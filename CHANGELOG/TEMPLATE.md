# Surface history entry template

Describe the developer or user job, the source-backed problem, the change and its proof limits. Record only work that actually happened; do not invent earlier entries or an unknown commit hash.

```md
# <Surface> history

## YYYY-MM-DD — Short imperative title

What changed and why. State the before/after observation and what it does not prove.

**Commit**: <verified commit or uncommitted candidate>. **Author**: <name>.
**Touches**: <related lane/index links>.
```
